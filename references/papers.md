# 核心论文清单

按章节组织，各章推荐 2-3 篇必读论文（第 4 章、第 13 章暂无）。

## 第 1-2 章：Agent 基础与 Harness

- [Google Agents 白皮书](https://www.kaggle.com/whitepaper-agents) — Agent 基础概念
- [A Survey of Self-Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21046) — 自进化 Agent 综述（77 页，TMLR 2026）

## 第 3 章：核心模式

- [ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) — ReAct 模式
- [Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366) — Reflexion 模式
- [Plan-and-Solve Prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04091) — 规划模式

## 第 5 章：推理能力

- [Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903) — CoT
- [Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601) — ToT
- [Graph of Thoughts: Solving Elaborate Problems with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09687) — GoT

## 第 6 章：规划与决策

- [SagaLLM: Context Management, Validation, and Transaction Guarantees for Multi-Agent LLM Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11951) — 事务性规划
- [Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Software Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14499) — METR 任务长度指标

## 第 7 章：Context Engineering

- [A Survey of Context Engineering for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13334) — Context Engineering 综述
- [Sleep-time Compute](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13171) — 离线计算

## 第 8 章：记忆系统

- [Memory in the Age of AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13564) — Agent 记忆综述
- [A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11432) — LLM Agent 总论（记忆为其架构组件之一）

## 第 9 章：工具使用

- [Gorilla: Large Language Model Connected with Massive APIs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15334) — 工具使用
- [Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761) — 自动工具学习

## 第 10 章：Agent 评估

- [SWE-bench: Can Language Models Resolve Real-World GitHub Issues?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770) — 代码 Agent 评估
- [WebArena: A Realistic Web Environment for Building Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13854) — Web Agent 评估
- [GAIA: A Benchmark for General AI Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12983) — 通用 Agent 评估

## 第 11 章：安全与对齐

- [Not what you've signed up for: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12173) — Prompt Injection
- [Agentic AI Safety & Security](https://rdi.berkeley.edu/agentic-ai/f25) — Berkeley CS294 安全课

## 第 12 章：自进化

- [A Survey of Self-Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21046) — 综述（what/when/how/where to evolve）
- [Large Language Models as Analogical Reasoners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01714) — 类比推理

## 第 14 章：应用前沿

- [The Virtual Lab of AI agents designs new SARS-CoV-2 nanobodies](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09442-9) — 科学发现
